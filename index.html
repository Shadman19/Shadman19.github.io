Can we tell when a deployed model should not be trusted?

I work on calibration and uncertainty under distribution shift, adversarial robustness, and privacy-preserving machine learning, mostly in medical AI, where a confident wrong answer is costly. My papers link to their code, and my demos show the systems side.

Research

Three threads, one question: knowing when a model's output can be relied on, and fixing it when it cannot.

Calibration under shift

Recalibrating a deployed model on encrypted features

A model that drifts at a new hospital is corrected on CKKS-encrypted features, so the hospital shares no raw data, logits or gradients. The work also showed that the approximations that make encryption practical can quietly distort a model's confidence even when it still separates classes well.

IEEE ICNC 2026 · about 2 s per correction on a CPU, 64-dimensional features

Adversarial robustness

How segmentation models fail when someone wants them to

A learned noise patch makes a brain tumor segmentation U-Net suppress the tumor mask, turning a visible lesion into a false negative. The point is the failure mode in a safety-critical setting.

IEEE ICCIT 2023

Reliable medical AI

Flagging inputs and cases a model should not handle alone

Per-lead ECG quality estimation trained on synthetic corruption labels and evaluated on PTB-XL, BUT QDB and CinC 2011; uncertainty for flagging tumors a CT detector misses, evaluated on 1,948 external scans; selective deferral of molecular tests under tissue-source shift; encrypted pooled recalibration across ICU hospitals.

Manuscripts in preparation; code released for the ECG and eICU studies

Post-training

DPO reliability audit

DPO with LoRA on Qwen2.5-0.5B across a beta sweep: 0.66 to 0.70 held-out preference accuracy, but most low-beta reward gain came from longer answers, and confidence rose as accuracy fell with beta. A companion study shows safety judges calibrated on ordinary prompts break on jailbreaks.

Encrypted post-deployment calibration

Reference implementation for the ICNC 2026 paper. Only the final classifier layer is updated, under CKKS encryption, with a Chebyshev approximation of softmax. Notebooks for PathMNIST, PneumoniaMNIST and MIMIC-III.
